# Glossary of Stock Market Terms

## A–D

- **AMO (after-market order)**: an order placed outside market hours, sent at the next open.
- **ASBA**: Application Supported by Blocked Amount; the IPO application method where money is blocked, not debited, until allotment.
- **Bear market**: a fall of 20% or more from a recent high, with pessimism. **Bull market**: a sustained rise.
- **Blue chip**: a large, well-established, financially strong company.
- **Book value**: net worth (assets minus liabilities) per share.
- **Bracket / cover order**: intraday orders with an attached stop-loss (offered by some brokers).
- **CAGR**: compound annual growth rate; the constant yearly rate that takes a starting value to an ending value.
- **Circuit (upper/lower)**: the daily price band limit for a stock.
- **CNC**: cash-and-carry; the delivery product for investing.
- **Correction**: a fall of 10% to 20% from a recent high.
- **Demat account**: holds shares electronically at NSDL or CDSL.
- **DII / FII (FPI)**: domestic institutional investors / foreign portfolio investors.

## E–M

- **EBITDA**: earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation.
- **EPS**: earnings per share.
- **Ex-date**: the date from which a buyer is no longer entitled to a declared dividend or corporate action.
- **Face value**: the nominal value of a share (for example ₹1, ₹2, ₹10); unrelated to the market price.
- **Free float**: shares available for public trading (excluding promoter holdings); index weights use free-float market cap.
- **Large / mid / small cap**: companies ranked 1–100 / 101–250 / 251+ by market capitalisation.
- **LTP**: last traded price.
- **Market capitalisation**: share price × total shares outstanding.
- **MIS**: margin intraday square-off; positions closed the same day.

## N–R

- **NAV**: net asset value per unit of a mutual fund.
- **NPA**: non-performing asset; a loan in default for 90+ days.
- **Order book**: all pending buy (bid) and sell (ask) orders for a stock.
- **P&L (realised / unrealised)**: realised profit or loss is locked in by selling; unrealised is on open positions at the current price.
- **Day P&L**: change in the value of current holdings since the previous close (or since purchase for shares bought today).
- **Pledge**: promoters using their shares as loan collateral.
- **Promoter**: founder or controlling shareholder of a listed company.
- **Rally**: a sharp rise in prices.
- **Record date**: the date on which shareholders must be registered to receive a corporate benefit.
- **Rupee cost averaging**: buying a fixed rupee amount regularly so more units are bought when prices are low.

## S–Z

- **SEBI**: Securities and Exchange Board of India, the market regulator.
- **Settlement (T+1)**: shares and money are exchanged one trading day after the trade.
- **SIP**: systematic investment plan; fixed monthly investment into a mutual fund.
- **Slippage**: the difference between the expected and actual fill price.
- **Spread**: difference between the best bid and best ask.
- **Stop-loss**: an order that exits a position when price reaches a trigger.
- **STT**: Securities Transaction Tax, charged on exchange trades.
- **Volatility**: how much prices fluctuate; often measured by standard deviation of returns.
- **Volume**: number of shares traded in a period.
- **Yield (dividend)**: annual dividend divided by price.
- **52-week high/low**: the highest and lowest price over the past year.
